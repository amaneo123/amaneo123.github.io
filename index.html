In the shattered seas of Deepwoken — where monsters, madness, and metal clashed endlessly — legends were not born; they were forged. One such legend was Amaneo123, a warrior whose name echoed across the depths like a storm.

But before Amaneo stood at the summit, there were only two: Glores and Xeno — and, from the early age, one important comrade: Tim. These were among the first to truly fight in Deepwoken.

Back in the early days, Lower Erisia looked like a beginner's island — yet for these three it was a battlefield. Voidwalkers ruled the lands. Bandits held the camps. Every campfire was a trap. The servers were warzones. The Depths were death sentences.

In that chaos, Glores and Xeno stood their path.

Chapter I — The First Blades

They Were Not Handed Strength

No legendaries. No rare bells. They started like everyone else — bare fists, broken armor, and a dream of surviving one more fight. But their ambition was different.

Glores

The Goat · Perfect Control

He trained in silence, studying every parry, every swing. Disciplined — a master of timing and precision. Where others spammed mantras, Glores waited for the right moment and made every strike matter.

Xeno

The Storm · Katana

Graceful but lethal, sharpened like a blade through wind. He trained for speed and unpredictability — one dash, three slashes, a whole squad down before they could react. An artist painting chaos with steel.

Tim

The Mountain-Breaker · Power

He didn't vanish into shadows — he stood his ground. Heavy weapons swung like they weighed nothing, paired with devastating mantras: fire blasts, shockwaves, lightning that cracked the sky. He tanked hits and answered with force that shattered mountains.

They met by fate, bonded through pain. The trio didn't always agree — Tim's recklessness clashed with Glores' structure, and Xeno often vanished mid-training to chase his obsessions — but they understood each other the way only survivors can.

⚔
Chapter II — The Guild

The Bw Bros

It started as a joke: three brainless freshies standing over the campfire in Lower Erisia, near the bandit camp — clothes torn, hands bruised — and a new guild called the Bw Bros.

BW — short for BedWars. A name once whispered with awe across a completely different universe: Hypixel. Back then they weren't survivors — they were champions. An overpowered trio famous for synergy, tactics, and dominance. The Bw Bros weren't just good. They were feared.

They built the guild brick by brick. And then Tim disappeared. No word. No message.

Glores and Xeno waited. Days turned to weeks. Weeks to months. But they kept training. Lower Erisia no longer held challenge or value, so they moved to Starswept Valley — a place that demanded more, and rewarded those who could survive its chaos. For four months, just the two of them: threshers, sand knights, golems. Learning the terrain. Mastering new mantras.

⚔
Chapter III — Bonds Reforged

The Reunion of Gren

It was a random encounter that felt like fate. In the middle of nowhere in Starswept Valley, they stumbled across a familiar name — Grencel · David · Gren · Beher1t. An old friend. A combat warrior who had fought beside them years before, wielding advanced weaponry with a style precise and brutal.

At first, not much was said. A nod. A "yo." Then Gren grew comfortable, and joined. It wasn't just two anymore — it was three again, but different. Stronger. Focused. Each trained not to catch up, but to lift the others higher.

And then Tim returned. He showed up in Starswept wielding his orange Petras Anchor, still scarred from the petrification of Deepwoken. "'Sup," he said. They were stunned. The Bw Bros weren't a joke anymore — they were better than ever. That was the moment the Bw Bros stopped being an idea… and became a brotherhood.

Chapter IV — The Outsider

And Here Comes Zeli

It began mid-battle. Zeli found Xeno caught in a fight and stepped in, facing every spin-back thrown against them. As the odds turned, Xeno turned to face the man who had saved him. Zeli didn't offer many words — he simply watched. An alliance formed.

Battle after battle, Zeli proved himself — not just in strength, but in loyalty. And the others proved themselves to him. No one questioned his past or his motives. He was accepted through action, through scars, through silent acknowledgment in the aftermath of every victory.

There was no announcement. No formal invitation. Zeli simply became part of the guild — naturally, seamlessly. Not because he asked, but because it was where he fit. In the end, he was no longer the outsider. He was one of them.

"They weren't fighters he stood beside. They had become his brothers."
Chapter V — The Forge

The Making of Amaneo

Amaneo began as an outcast, wandering aimlessly through the wildlife of Etris. Physically strong, but without purpose — a homeless man who fought only for the thrill of combat. That is, until he crossed paths with Glores, the Deepwoken veteran known only as The Goat.

Glores didn't speak much, but what he taught echoed in every ounce of Amaneo's purpose. "gopmop." The training was brutal. Amaneo was broken down and remolded — taught that patience, precision, and pain are what forge a true fighter. He learned to weave mantras with precision, to fight not for pride but for purpose. Glores handed him a custom weapon: the Soulthorne — proof that a fight is not only about the sharpness of your blade or the power of your mantras, but the adaptation and inner peace born of wielding your own weapon.

But Glores wasn't the only one who shaped him.

Xeno

Gave · Speed

Chaos in motion. Training with Xeno meant adapting to unpredictability — to react without thinking, to move as if the body understood the battlefield before the mind did.

Gren

Gave · Vision

The brutal tactician. Every movement deliberate, every choice calculated. Gren taught him to see the entire fight — the enemy, the environment, the rhythm. To think like a warrior, not just hit like one.

Tim

Gave · Fury

Pure devastation, his Petras Anchor flying like Mjölnir. Where Glores taught control, Tim reminded him not to fear his own strength — that survival sometimes demands destruction.

Together, the four became more than comrades — they became a forge. And Amaneo was the blade within it. Where others broke under pressure, he adapted. Where others hesitated, he moved forward. He absorbed everything — Glores' patience, Xeno's speed, Gren's precision, Tim's power — and turned it into something new. Something terrifying.

He wasn't just part of their story anymore. He was the story. He became the legacy. He rose.

He was proof that adaptation was the success of the Bw Bros.
Chapter VI — Ascension

The Rise of Alyx Trigi

Amaneo stepped into the world of chime — a new arena of elite warriors and ruthless competition. He didn't dominate immediately, but he learned, and fast. Each loss became power, teaching him his weaknesses. Each win, a stepping stone. Top 250. Then top 100. Then top 50 — not once, but three times.

Every match was war. Every gank he joined became an omen. When Amaneo arrived, the Voidheart shivered. Guilds logged. People logged. Some recognized his weapon. Others only saw the name Alyx Trigi — and panicked.

He wasn't a fighter anymore. He was a force — a part of the elements. People didn't just die when Amaneo fought. They were crushed. He didn't need an army. He was one. He was the reason the name Bw Bros meant something feared across the servers.

Chapter VII — The Fall

Eviscerate

It happened on a day like any other — training in Hive, friendly fights. Then the Bw Bros — Glores, Zeli, Xeno, Tim, and Gren — were ambushed by a guild known as Eviscerate: overwhelming numbers, merciless tactics. They didn't fight to win. They fought to erase.

Outnumbered, surrounded, outpaced — even they couldn't hold. Glores stood at the frontline until his blade shattered. Xeno regenerated his mantras and fought on. Gren fell back-to-back with Tim, both refusing to fall quietly. Zeli took out three on his own before they overwhelmed him.

But they lost. And Alyx Trigi wasn't there.

By the time word reached him, the damage was done. His brothers — injured, broken, humiliated. Not dead. But defeated. The guilt was unbearable. After everything they'd done for him, he hadn't just missed a fight. He'd let them down. He'd failed the people who made him.

But Amaneo didn't let guilt drown him. He turned it into vengeance. And he had a plan.

Chapter VIII — The Long Knife

The Infiltration

He disappeared. The boys assumed he was training again, or grinding toward another top-50 run. But behind the scenes, he was infiltrating. Amaneo tried out for Eviscerate. He masked himself, buried the memory of what they'd done to his friends, and played the part of a cold, efficient fighter. They welcomed him — his strength was undeniable.

For three months he worked alongside his enemies. Training with them. Joking with them. All the while collecting every detail.

And then the moment arrived.
Chapter IX — Retribution

Soulthorne

He called it a ganking mission — told Eviscerate's higher-ups a major guild was attacking his members in the Depths, and that he'd devised a plan to jump them. The leader believed him. Trusted him.

When the time came, two lieutenants arrived first. But Amaneo stood alone. No smile. Just silence — and Soulthorne in his grip.

They recognized the blade first. The weapon gifted by Glores. The weapon that marked him not as a recruit — but as a brother of the Bw Bros. Realization hit just before the steel did.

He moved fast, no hesitation. One lieutenant fell before drawing a weapon. The second tried to run — cut down mid-turn. No mercy. No pause. They weren't comrades. They were enemies.

And then the leader arrived. What followed was a monstrosity that shook the Depths. But Amaneo was something else. He wasn't fighting for himself — he was fighting for Glores, for Xeno, for Zeli, for Tim, for Gren. Using everything they taught him to fell this monster.

And then the final m1 came. Soulthorne drove straight through the leader's chest in a burst of fury. No final words. Just silence. Just victory.

Blood in his eyes, seeing red — he wasn't the same as before. He was unleashed. Eviscerate had tried to break the Bw Bros. Instead, they unleashed its true potential.
